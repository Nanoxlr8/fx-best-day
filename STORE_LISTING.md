**Full description:**

## Stop converting money on bad days.

FX Best-Day watches your currency pair and tells you the one thing that matters:
**is today a good day to move your money, or should you wait a day or two?**

Perfect for:
- 💼 Converting a **USD salary** to local currency
- 🌍 **Freelancers** paid in foreign currency
- 🏠 **Remittances** sent home
- 📈 Anyone tired of guessing the rate

## How it works
1. Pick your pair (e.g. USD → EGP).
2. It fetches the live rate (free, no account needed).
3. It quietly logs the rate each day and builds a **7-day average, high, and low**.
4. You get a plain verdict:
   - 🟢 **Convert today — near the peak**
   - 🟢 **Reasonable to convert today**
   - 🟠 **Worth waiting a day or two**

Turn on notifications and it pings you only on good days.

## Free vs Pro

**Free (Lite)** — everything most people need:
- Track any currency pair
- 7-day average / high / low
- "Best day this week" verdict
- History export/import

**Pro** — for people who move money across multiple currencies or want it automated:
- 🔥 **Multi-pair watchlist** — track USD→EGP, GBP→EGP, EUR→USD side by side
- 🔥 **Daily Telegram digest** — wake up to a private message: which pair to convert today
- Custom alert threshold

Pro availability and purchase details can be provided through the extension's official listing.
